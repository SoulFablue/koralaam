What happens in between?

Letters become UTF-8 bytes, then bits. Recovery sends each bit three times; the receiver takes the majority vote. Noise flips bits at the selected probability. A CRC-16 checksum travels with the message and checks the result. Too much noise can defeat recovery. Replies are prewritten and appear only after a matching, verified message arrives.
